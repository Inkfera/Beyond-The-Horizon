## Inspiration and code origin

Bloomtype is built on the idea and the drawing/growth approach of **Type Garden** by
**Akshat Agarwal** (<https://type-garden.vercel.app>). The growth, rose-drawing, leaf and butterfly
routines in `app.js` are adapted from the source code of that project.
